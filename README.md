# AI Grant Writer & Incentive Optimizer (PoC) 🚀

### Strategic Automation for High-Stakes Public Funding

This project is a **Proof of Concept (PoC)** demonstrating how multi-agent AI frameworks can bridge the gap between complex government regulations and corporate funding needs. By leveraging **CrewAI and LangChain**, I have architected a system that automates the lifecycle of grant applications (hibe), significantly reducing operational lead times.

## 🎯 The Problem
In emerging markets like **Turkey and Serbia**, companies often miss out on vital green transition grants due to:
- **Complexity:** Navigating thousands of pages of regulatory requirements (TSRS, CSRD, etc.).
- **Resources:** Small teams spending hundreds of hours on repetitive documentation.
- **Accuracy:** High failure rates due to minor data misalignments.

## 💡 The Solution: Agentic Workflow
This framework utilizes a decentralized "Agentic" approach where specialized AI agents collaborate:
1. **The Researcher:** Scans regulatory databases and matches client profiles with eligible grants.
2. **The Analyst:** Extracts key KPIs (Carbon footprint, financial status) from corporate documents.
3. **The Writer:** Drafts the formal application, ensuring 100% alignment with government templates.

## 🛠 Tech Stack
- **Frameworks:** LangChain, CrewAI
- **Language:** Python
- **Intelligence:** OpenAI / Anthropic API integration
- **Goal:** Transforming "Regulatory Burden" into "Operational Advantage."
